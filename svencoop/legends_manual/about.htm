What is Land of Legends ?
Land of Legends is a sub-mod based on Sven Co-op, which is a part of the Half-Life community. In Legends, players take on the role of a group of hero's in a medieval fantasy world. It's possible to play Legends on your own or with friends. For the optimal experience we recommend 2 to 4 players.

Legends has been created by the DevEd.
