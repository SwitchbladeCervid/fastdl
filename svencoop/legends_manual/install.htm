Installation

To play Land of Legends, you need a STEAM Half-Life installation. Note that we do not support the old WON system. Make sure you've run Half-Life at least once after installing it on Steam, so it creates the required folder structure.

Installing Land of Legends
1. After downloading Land of Legends, run the .exe or .zip-file
2. Extract everything to your Half-Life folder. You can find this folder in /Steam/steamapps/youraccountname/half-life/. If you have trouble finding your Steam folder: on default it can be found in C:/Program Files(x86)/Steam/ .
3. Restart Steam and you'll find Land of Legends in your Games list. Double click and launch the application.
