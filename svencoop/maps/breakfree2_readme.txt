Breakfree 2 - By Beather

Dr Robotnik (Eggman) has captured you and your friends!
You're forced to move on a moving platform dodging 
rotating blocks, high-speed rotating blades,
suprise-moving walls, and much more. Meet 
Dr. Robotnik at the end in a built-in boss battle.

However it looks familiar, Breakfree 2 has a totally different
system. When you die, you don't have to wait the whole level,
but you have to wait until the "Play" gate opens, which
happens quite frequently.

I tried to put more detail on the texturing and
architecture, so I hope you like it...
Lots of new sounds, taken from sonic 1 and 2,
the music got improved with the looping and
new monster models!

Special thanks to ZpElmO, without his great model work
this map wouldn't be possible :D
